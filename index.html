FAQ

Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before you start.

How much does interior design cost for a 2, 3 or 4 BHK in Bangalore?

A 2 BHK typically starts around ₹4 lakhs, a 3 BHK around ₹6 lakhs and a 4 BHK around ₹8 lakhs, depending on the package and scope.

How much does a modular kitchen cost?

Modular kitchens start from around ₹2 lakhs. Price depends on layout, size, materials and appliances.

Can you design for a small kitchen?

Yes. We use tall units, corner carousels and wall storage to get the most out of compact kitchens.

How long does it take to complete the interiors?

Most projects are delivered within 45 days of design sign-off.

What services do you offer?

Full home interiors, modular kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings, lighting, painting, and decor.

How can I get a quote for my home interiors?

Book a consultation with our designers. After understanding your space and needs, we share a detailed, itemised quote based on your final 3D design.

What common mistakes should I avoid?

Skipping storage planning, choosing finishes without samples, and ignoring lighting are the most common ones. Your designer will guide you.
